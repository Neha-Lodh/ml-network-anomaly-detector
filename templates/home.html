📊 Data Cleaning

Uploaded CSV traffic logs are validated, deduplicated, and cleaned automatically before analysis.

🤖 Dual ML Models

Isolation Forest spots statistical outliers, while Random Forest classifies traffic using learned patterns.

🎯 Risk Scoring

Every record gets a 0-100 risk score and a Low / Medium / High label, with a human-readable reason.

🗄️ MySQL Storage

All traffic records and detected alerts are stored securely using parameterized SQL queries.

📈 Live Dashboard

Interactive Chart.js visualizations show traffic volume, protocol mix, and risk distribution.

🔔 Alerts

A dedicated Alerts page lists every anomaly, filterable by risk level, with review status tracking.
